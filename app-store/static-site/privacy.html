飞行记录隐私政策

生效日期：2026-06-22

概述

飞行记录是一款离线优先的 iPad 工具，用于记录飞行联络和巡航检查信息。本应用不需要登录账号，不包含广告，不包含第三方分析 SDK，也不会将记录自动上传给开发者或第三方。

数据存储

用户在应用中填写的飞行记录、机长档案号和备份数据保存在用户自己的 iPad 本地。除非用户主动使用 iPadOS 分享、导出备份或导入备份功能，否则这些数据不会离开设备。

数据收集

本应用不收集、追踪、出售或共享用户个人数据。应用不会为广告或分析目的收集设备标识符、位置、使用行为或诊断数据。

联网与备案说明

App 本体不提供互联网信息服务，不需要后端服务器、账号系统、同步服务或运行域名。GitHub Pages 页面只用于 App Store 公开支持和隐私政策展示，不属于 App 的功能服务或运行依赖。基于当前离线本地工具设计，App 运行本体没有需要 ICP/APP 备案的互联网服务。

备份与删除

用户可以在应用内导出备份文件，并自行保存到文件 App、iCloud Drive 或其他位置。用户可以通过删除应用来删除保存在应用沙盒内的本地数据。

联系我们

如需隐私相关支持，请访问 飞行记录支持页，或发送邮件至 zhiyuan_1996@outlook.com。

Flight Recorder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-22

Overview

Flight Recorder is an offline-first iPad utility for flight communication and cruise-check records. The app does not require an account, does not include advertising, does not include third-party analytics SDKs, and does not automatically upload records to the developer or any third party.

Data Storage

Flight records, captain archive numbers, and backup data entered by the user are stored locally on the user's iPad. The data does not leave the device unless the user chooses to share, export, or import backup files through iPadOS.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data. It does not collect device identifiers, location, usage behavior, or diagnostics for advertising or analytics.

Network and Filing Position

The app itself does not provide an internet information service and does not require a backend server, account system, sync service, or runtime domain. The GitHub Pages website is only used for public App Store support and privacy-policy display; it is not part of the app functionality or runtime dependency. Based on the current offline local-utility design, the app runtime has no internet service that requires ICP/app filing.

Backup and Deletion

Users can export backup files from the app and choose where to store them, such as the Files app, iCloud Drive, or another location. Users can delete local app data by deleting the app from the iPad.

Contact

For privacy support, visit Flight Recorder Support or email zhiyuan_1996@outlook.com.
